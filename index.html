Happy Valentine’s Day

To my wife & best friend

My love, Happy Valentine’s Day to my wife, my best friend, my constant, and the only girl who has known me since junior kindergarten. It still amazes me that our story did not begin with some dramatic first meeting. It began with crayons, school uniforms, and two kids who had no idea they would grow up and choose each other forever. You used to bully me in 5th grade, and somehow that teasing never really stopped. It just changed. It became love hiding inside sarcasm, affection wrapped in eye rolls, and care disguised as the way you still pull my leg. And I would not change a single second of it. Because underneath that teasing girl has always been the woman who stands by me, challenges me, and pushes me to become better. You have seen every version of me. The awkward kid. The confused teenager. The dreaming man trying to build something of himself. And you stayed. You did not fall in love with just one phase of me. You grew with me. That is what makes us special. We do not just share romance. We share history. We share classrooms, growing pains, silly fights, uncontrollable laughter, and inside jokes that nobody else would ever understand. We share a lifetime that started long before either of us understood what love even meant. Today I do not just celebrate Valentine’s Day. I celebrate us. I celebrate the way you still tease me. I celebrate the comfort I feel with you. I celebrate that out of everyone in this world, we somehow found our way back to each other. I promise to keep choosing you. In every season. In every phase. In every lifetime.
Forever yours bachha,
Ethan
